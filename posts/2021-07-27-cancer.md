Cancer is a weird thing.

It's your own body mutating and attacking you.

The word is Latin for "Crab". I guess it has to do with the way a cancer cell looks like, as opposed to healthy cells. I also guess it's one of the fun facts you pick up when combing through Wikipedia articles to figure out what's wrong with you.

On May 21st I was diagnosed with a testicular tumor that had to be removed ASAP. The next day I was on the operating table. It was later determined that I had stage 1C embryonal carcinoma. The cancer was pumping out enough fetal growth hormone (beta-HCG) to peg me at 5 weeks pregnant.
