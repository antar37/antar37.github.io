It's easy to [write about consistency](/c5b136fe99264c378d79974b362a21e4), it's hard to be consistent.

I've moved my platform to Notion. Why?

Because the main thing I've wanted to do is get into the habit of writing. What has been keeping me from doing that?

Friction.

Friction when it comes to creating the post, moving it to markdown, and deploying.

I know how to develop, and design. That's not the problem. The problem is that I haven't been able to get into the groove of simply writing, due to the additional friction I've created for myself by using other platforms.

**What do I lose?** Lots of stuff.

- I lose SEO

- I lose control over layout

- I lose the ability to schedule posts

**What do I gain?**

- Exclusive focus on content

*And that makes all the difference.*

## The takeaway

Charles Duhigg writes about "keystone habits" in his book "[The Power of Habit](https://www.amazon.com/Power-Habit-What-Life-Business/dp/081298160X)". A keystone habit is one that hooks in a bunch of other habits that eventually change lots of other habits. In his book, he highlights a new CEO that prioritized safety over everything else. As a CEO, there are LOTS of things to think about other than safety, things like profitability and hiring and workplace culture. However, by focusing single-mindedly on safety, the CEO was able to upgrade machinery, improve processes, and build a culture that attracted high-quality hires. His single-minded focus ended up impacting every area of the company. The result is a profitable, healthy company.

In order to do something that becomes part of your routine, remove all friction and focus on that ONE thing single-mindedly. Let everything else fall into place in its own time.

*That's what I'm going to attempt.*
